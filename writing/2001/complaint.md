**Subject: Lack of Allergen-Friendly Food Options on Weekends**

Dear Joshua Mitchell,

I am writing to express my serious concern about the inadequate allergen-friendly food options available in the college dining hall during weekends. As a student with food allergies who relies on the meal plan I have paid for, I have consistently found that safe food options are unavailable on Saturdays and Sundays.

During the weekdays, the dining hall provides clearly labeled allergen-friendly meals that allow me to eat safely. However, beginning each Friday evening and continuing through Sunday, these options disappear entirely. This means I am left without access to the dining services I have paid for through my mandatory meal plan, forcing me to either skip meals or purchase food off-campus at my own additional expense.

I have already spoken with dining hall staff on multiple occasions and submitted feedback through the online system, but the issue remains unresolved. This problem has caused significant inconvenience, additional financial burden, and genuine concern for my health and safety. As a student managing coursework and other responsibilities, having reliable access to safe food throughout the entire week is essential.

I would appreciate it if you could ensure that allergen-friendly food options are available seven days a week in the dining hall, consistent with the weekday offerings. I request a response outlining how and when this matter will be addressed, as it affects my ability to safely utilize the meal plan services I am required to purchase.

I have documented specific dates and meals when allergen-friendly options were unavailable and can provide this information if needed.

Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to your prompt response and a resolution that ensures all students with dietary restrictions have access to safe food options throughout the entire week.
