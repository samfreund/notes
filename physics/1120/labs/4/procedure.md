# 1a
1. Place straight parallel electrodes 7" apart in the tray.
2. 10 volt AC to the electrodes, check voltage.
3. Connect AC output terminals to common terminal on DMM.
4. Probe left electrode: 0, probe right: 10V.
5. Draw tray on graph paper
6. Find equipotential lines for 1V, 2V, 3V, 4V, 5V.
7. By symmetry, copy quadrant and draw equipotential lines.
