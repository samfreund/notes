1. Does your data line up with the fit line?
With an R² value of 0.9953, the data shows excellent alignment with the linear fit.
The points closely follow the theoretical prediction.

2. Does the fit line describe the system?
Yes, the linear relationship between mg and (2Lf/n)² validates equation (6): mg = μ(2Lf/n)².
The slope of the fit line represents the linear density μ = 0.002400 kg/m.
Note: There is a small intercept of -0.0187 N, which would ideally be zero.

3. Where might errors have been introduced?
Potential sources of error include:

Node identification: Difficult to precisely identify nodes, especially for higher n values where nodes are closer together and amplitude is smaller
Frequency stability: The function generator may drift
String length measurement: Measuring vibrating string length with system off introduces uncertainty because the string can stretch
Resonance tuning: Subjective determination of maximum amplitude at resonance
